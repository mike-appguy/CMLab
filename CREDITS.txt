CM LAB - COMMUNITY AND RESEARCH REFERENCES

CM Lab is an unofficial companion. Championship Manager and third-party project
names belong to their respective owners. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.
No game executable, game database, crest pack, player save or browser profile is
included in this release.

Research and reference tools acknowledged by this project:
- CM Scout and the CM 01/02 scouting community
- GPF2 and slot-based regen tracking
- CM Scout Intrinsic Community:
  https://github.com/MScientistCM/CMScoutIntrinsicCommunity
- CM0102 research and benchmarking projects:
  https://github.com/agevak/CM0102
  https://github.com/agevak/CM0102PlayerBenchmarker
- Championship Manager 01/02 community forum guides and the original manual

TonTon is named in appreciation of Tonton Zola Moukoko and the search for similarly
memorable players. Model weights remain experimental. The source audit records that external formula code, benchmark coefficients and
research datasets are not incorporated into the generated app. Research copies
remain outside the release. Future direct third-party implementation or dataset
reuse requires its own permission/licence review; references are not licences.
