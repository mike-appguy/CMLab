# CM Lab

A local companion for **Championship Manager 01/02**, built from years of playing, community research, forum posts and notebook notes.

## Download

Download **CM-Lab-3.1-RC1.zip** from [Releases](https://github.com/mike-appguy/CMLab/releases). Extract it to a permanent folder and double-click **CM Lab.cmd**. Windows and Microsoft Edge are required. No account or subscription is needed to use CM Lab.

CM Lab opens in its own Edge app window without normal browser tabs or an address bar. It works locally and offline. **It never edits your game save or installation.**

## What's inside

- **Scouting and player profiles:** detailed filters, hidden attributes, contract information, loan ownership and comparisons.
- **Find the next…:** discover younger, less established players with similar strengths and development headroom.
- **TonTon:** named after Tonton Zola Moukoko, this experimental model searches for overlooked players with exceptional attribute combinations.
- **Squad depth and Best XI:** review positional quality, build a custom tactic and calculate a unique lineup using position-specific LabLogic ratings.
- **Training:** assess coaching assignments, plan eight shared programmes and record your manual in-game choices.
- **Development:** compare imported saves to see changes in ability and attributes.
- **Regen Centre:** track reused slots against your career reference, with first-seen dates and contract details.
- **Shortlist and War Room:** organise targets, notes, priorities and replacement comparisons.

## Your data

Load your own `.sav`; no players, personal careers or databases are bundled. The first import establishes the career's regen reference. Import later saves into that same history; create a separate history for a different career.

Use **Workspace & backup** regularly. Before changing browser/profile, computer or app location, download a complete `.cmlab` backup and restore it in the new workspace. Keep the original until you have verified the transfer. Never upload private saves or workspace backups to public issues.

The historical filename `CM_Lab_v0.3.html` is retained for storage continuity; the visible version is **3.1 RC1**. Keep your installation folder stable when updating.

## Release status

This is a **release candidate**, not universal compatibility certification. Development tests include a modern CM 01/02 database with Nick's Patcher. Other databases, patches and save variants need broader testing. LabLogic and TonTon are scouting models, not guaranteed match-engine predictions; training is advisory and applied manually.
