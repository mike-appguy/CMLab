What to know before you start

Storage, updates and moving to another computer

Your app window uses its own Edge profile. Chrome, another Edge profile, a moved app folder or another PC cannot automatically see that data. Use Download complete backup, then restore that file in the new workspace. Keep this installation path stable when updating. Clearing browser/app storage can remove locally held data. Clearing the saved import keeps career snapshots; a complete backup also protects your current imported save.

Compatibility and honest limitations

This release candidate supports the native compressed/uncompressed CM 01/02 save layouts implemented by the reader. Development testing uses a modern database with Nick’s Patcher; every patch/database combination has not been tested. A successful import is not a guarantee that every field matches the patched game display.

LabLogic is our scouting model. TonTon is an experimental attribute model. Neither predicts match results or proves a player is an exploit. Converted attributes use CM Lab’s shared 1–20 scale. Unknown values remain unknown. Training recommendations are advisory and must be applied manually in game; condition, injuries and actual in-game routine assignments are not reliably decoded.

Regen tracking compares reused slots, not biological links. A reference made halfway through a career cannot reconstruct regens that appeared before it. First seen means an imported save date, not a player creation date. The latest 12 development snapshots per career are retained; the regen reference and recorded first-seen history are kept separately.

Credits, research and community

CM Lab is an unofficial companion and is not affiliated with the game’s creators. Thanks to the CM 01/02 community and the work documented by CM Scout, GPF2, CM Scout Intrinsic Community and the CM0102 research projects. Names acknowledge the research behind the project; they do not imply endorsement or that every community theory is verified.

Reporting a problem

Include CM Lab 3.1 RC1, the in-game version, database/patch setup, save date, what you clicked and the exact message. Screenshots help. Keep a workspace backup and an untouched game save. Share save or backup files privately only if you are comfortable sharing the career data they contain. There is no automatic upload, telemetry or error reporting.

How the model works and what is verified

Capacity = (Natural Fitness + max(Stamina, Strength)) / 2. CM Lab uses a configurable safety margin and age / injury-proneness precautions, then a soft workload penalty. Marginal excess does not automatically disqualify a suitable routine. Exactly eight shared programmes are used; no individual variants are generated. Fitness costs 2/4/8 for Light/Medium/Intensive; other areas cost 1/2/4. None costs zero. This community heuristic is not a verified injury formula or a hard safety threshold.

Role priorities and weaknesses rank sustainable presets. Fixed PA is never increased or promised. Direct-training groups follow John North's engine investigation, and remain experimental for Nick's patched game. Attributes outside those groups receive no direct-training benefit. Reflexes are grouped under Fitness; Tackling under Skills.

The original manual describes routines and intensity choices. Your supplied game screenshot shows eight editable buttons plus No Training. Slot numbers here follow their visual order: General, Fitness, Shooting, Skill, Tactics, Gk, Unused, Unused. Rename/configure them manually; nothing is written to the game. Adjust the slot count if a different game version presents fewer buttons.

NF, Stamina, Strength, Injury Proneness and Determination use saved player/staff attributes; CA-dependent attributes use the established CM Lab conversion. Current condition, active injuries, match minutes, exact squad subgroup and observed training assignments are not reliably decoded. Missing evidence stays unavailable and recommendations use additional conservative margins where relevant.
