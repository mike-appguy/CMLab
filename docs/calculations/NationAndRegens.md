# National eligibility and regen tracking

## Nation

CM's saved international appearances field is documented as caps for the first nation: [RCM staff attributes](https://rcm.sourceforge.net/staff_attr.htm). CM Lab includes matching primary nationality; a matching second nationality is included only with a known zero recorded cap count. Capped-for-other and unknown-caps secondary candidates are excluded. Primary-only can be selected. Local exclusions use full player/staff/name identity.

Zero senior caps does not prove full eligibility. Youth ties, patch differences, international retirement, injuries and availability remain unverified. NationalJob is an employment field, not an allegiance override. These are conservative candidate pools, not decoded call-up squads. U21/U19 mean under 21/under 19 on the save date, not tournament birth-date cut-offs.

## Regens

Compare current player slot to this career's persistent reference: a changed occupant name is a slot-reuse candidate. This is GPF2-style identification, not proof of a biological or engine-defined relationship to the retired player. Reused IDs are not used alone to identify a person.

First seen is the earliest matching imported identity/date retained in the dedicated first-seen records or available snapshots. NEW means first seen on the current save date, not confirmed generation date. Dates are retained beyond the rolling twelve-snapshot development history.

Contract months remaining = (contract expiry − loaded save date) / 30.4375 days, displayed to one decimal; it is approximate. Parent contract expiry is used for loanees. Free agents, expired and unknown dates are labelled separately.

A late first reference cannot identify changes that already existed at that reference date. Preserve the original reference with complete workspace backups. Replacing a reference cannot invent historical first-seen dates; any restoration must validate career continuity and preserve the original backup. Public packages contain no personal baseline or saved career.
